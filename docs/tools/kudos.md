### Kudos

Kudos is a tool for writing CCDC inject responses (business requests) faster. Pick the inject type to get a template with the usual sections, tables, and screenshot slots, fill in the prompts, add your environment's hosts and screenshots, and export the finished memo as a `.docx` in a standard interoffice memo format.

**[Open Kudos]({{ '/kudos/' | relative_url }})**

Everything runs in the browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
